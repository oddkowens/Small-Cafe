Environmentally Minded

There's something we would like all of our Small Cafe guests to know.

Recycling and repurposing top the list of priorities at Small Cafe. 100% of our food waste and your plate leftovers go to help feed the Howerton Farm livestock, including their cute pigs.

So eat up, and don't worry about the extra bits left behind on your plate. It will not go to waste!
