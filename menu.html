Live large, eat Small

Using our local farmers and suppliers to come up with good homecooked meals, soup stocks that take a good 8 hours to create, and preserving the harvest like crazy so we get to eat good year round. Currently, close to 60% of everything that goes into a meal at the Small Cafe comes from local farmers and suppliers.

First Strawberries by Joseph Bruchac

Novels & Nibbles is sharing a traditional Cherokee folktale about strawberries, their symbol reconciliation, friendship, and sweetness. As the story goes the first man and woman's happiness is broken after a hurtful argument causes the woman to leave. The man follows after her wishing to apologize. The sun sees his sadness and places beautiful berries in the woman’s path hoping to slow her down, but only the sweet strawberries in the woman’s path catch her attention and remind her of the love they once shared. This tale is a timeless lesson about forgiveness, kindness, and how even small acts of sweetness can heal wounded hearts.

So, in honor of our book and strawberry season, we are serving house-made crepes filled with luscious mascarpone, topped with strawberries, silky dark chocolate ganache, and a dusting of powdered sugar. 

Pop in…give your taste buds this sweet little indulgence and their own happily ever after!
